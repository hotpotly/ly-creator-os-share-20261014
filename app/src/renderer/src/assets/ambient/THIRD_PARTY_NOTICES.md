# Ambient sound notices

These processed loop files are sourced from the open-source [Blankie](https://github.com/codybrom/blankie) ambient mixer.

- `rain.m4a` — “Rain” by alex36917, [Freesound 524605](https://freesound.org/people/alex36917/sounds/524605/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- `stream.m4a` — “Stream” by gluckose, [Freesound 333987](https://freesound.org/people/gluckose/sounds/333987/), dedicated to the public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- `fireplace.m4a` — “Fireplace” by the National Park Service and Montana State University Acoustic Atlas, public domain.

Blankie’s source metadata and normalization factors are retained in `ambient-audio.ts`. These notices do not change the license of LY Creator OS itself.
